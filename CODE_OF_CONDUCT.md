# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SkillSync to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

That matters doubly here: SkillSync exists to help people review candidates on their skills, fairly. We hold ourselves to the same standard.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning. This project started as a team learning project, and questions are always welcome.
- Respect different opinions and experiences, and give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, own your mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people using SkillSync and for the community.

## What's not okay

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Sharing someone's private information (including any resume or candidate data) without their permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and other channels), and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers at **kanamianderson@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential, and we won't tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for upholding this code. Depending on the situation, they may:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warn:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a while.
3. **Temporarily ban:** no interaction with the community for a set period.
4. **Permanently ban:** for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't follow this code.
